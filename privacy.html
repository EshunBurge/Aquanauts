Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Aquanauts is a learning game for kids ages 7 to 12. We collect as little as we can, and we never sell it. Here is the plain-language version of what that means.

What we collect

  • Account details. When a parent or guardian signs up, we store a name and an email address so the account can be found and kept safe.
  • Learning progress. Which missions are finished, quiz results, coins, and the crew pet a player has adopted.
  • Game settings. Things like the daily goal and sound choices.

We do not ask kids for their full name, address, school, phone number, or photo.

Where it is kept

Progress is saved in the browser on the device being used. When an account is connected, it may also be saved with our account provider (Google Firebase) so it can follow the player between devices. Clearing the browser's site data on a device removes the progress stored there.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent personal information.
  • We do not show ads, and we do not track kids across other websites.
  • We do not let kids chat with strangers. There is no open chat in Aquanauts.

The shop is a demo

The shop on this site is a demonstration. No payment is taken, no card details should be entered, and nothing is ordered or shipped.

Parents and guardians

Aquanauts is meant to be used with a parent or guardian's permission. A parent can ask to see, correct, or delete the information stored for their child's account at any time by contacting us below, and we will remove it.

Cookies and storage

We use the browser's local storage to remember a signed-in account, game progress, and the shopping cart. We do not use it for advertising.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: privacy@aquanauts.app
