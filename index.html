About the game

How the game works

Kids play as an astronaut living in the Aquanauts' world. The sea creature crew runs a base on the Moon, and each lesson gives you a problem to solve together. You read the story, learn a few new words, make a choice and see what happens.

1

Meet the crew

The crew lives and works on the Moon Base. Every character has a different job and a room of their own.

2

Play a lesson

Read the problem in a room, learn the key words, think it through and decide what to do.

3

Move on

Finish a lesson to earn a certificate and coins, then unlock the next room on the map.

Inside the game

Everything on your control center

When kids log in they land on their own control center. Here is what they can find there.

Adventures

Each adventure is a short story with a problem to solve. Read it, learn a few new words, then take a quick quiz.

Moon Base map

Every room is a spot on the map with a crew member inside. Finish one room to open the next, then tap any room to read about it.

Cantina

Win coins, badges and streak prizes as you play. Spend coins on crew pets that keep you company on your control center.

Certificates

Finish an adventure and print a certificate with your own name on it. Put it on the fridge or the classroom wall.

Real science

Where the science comes from

The problems in Aquanauts are based on real information from NASA. The crew turn it into missions kids can play.

DONKI

Space Weather Database

NASA keeps a diary of what the Sun is doing. It writes down solar flares and storms and says when they could be risky for astronauts.

LumaPebbleTink
OSDR

Open Science Data Repository

A giant library of space experiments. It shows how plants, animals and people change in space, and how much radiation astronauts really get.

BloopNoriLumaTink
Moon and Mars

Artemis and Mars surface missions

Robots and astronauts measure the ground, dust, air and temperature on the Moon and Mars, so we know what a real base has to survive.
