THE CHALLENGE

Building a junior astronaut mission trainer

Problem

Space exploration is often taught in one of two ways:

  1. Oversimplified. Students miss important concepts.
  2. Complex. Explained in language too technical for younger learners to understand.

As STEM students ourselves, we believe learning should strike a balance between accessibility and depth.

Oversimplification and complexity intertwined.

Solution

Aquanauts: an interactive simulation game that brings moon base living to life, with challenges to confront and solve.

Through hands-on missions inspired by NASA data, students navigate realistic scenarios that astronauts have faced or could encounter on the moon, developing decision-making skills along the way.

AQUANAUTS.

THE LESSONS

8 characters. 8 lessons. 8 levels. 8 certifications

Each lesson builds on the last, teaching the essential skills needed to operate a space outpost. By the final mission, students must apply everything they have learned to solve real-world challenges and make critical decisions to keep their crew safe.

01

Bloop

Keep your crew alive.

Learn to manage oxygen, air quality and water to keep astronauts safe in space.

02

Moss

Power the mission.

Discover how solar panels, batteries and smart energy decisions keep your mission running.

03

Nori

Grow beyond earth.

Learn to grow crops, manage a space greenhouse and keep your crew fed.

04

Luma

Shield your crew.

Explore the dangers of space and how to protect from radiation and environmental hazards.

05

Pip

Build the best base.

Design, test and repair essential systems to create a reliable space outpost.

06

Skim

Make every source count.

Discover how recycling and resource management help with outpost survival.

07

Pebble

Think ahead of schedule.

Make important decisions, prioritize tasks and prepare for the unexpected.

08

Tank

Multitasking.

The final commands. Put everything you have learned to the test.
