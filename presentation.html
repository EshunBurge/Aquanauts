THE CHALLENGE

Building a junior astronaut mission trainer

The problem

Space exploration is often taught in one of two ways: oversimplified to the point that students miss important concepts, or explained in language too technical for younger learners to understand.

As STEM students ourselves, we believe learning should strike a balance between accessibility and depth, making complex concepts both understandable and engaging.

The solution

To bridge this gap, we developed Aquanauts, an interactive astronaut simulation game that brings real-world space exploration challenges to life.

Through hands-on missions inspired by NASA data, students navigate realistic scenarios that astronauts have faced or could encounter in space, developing decision-making skills along the way.

Learn by doingPlayers repair real systems instead of memorizing facts.
Real scienceInformed by NASA DONKI and OSDR research.

THE LESSONS

8 characters, 8 lessons, 8 levels, 8 certifications

  • 01BloopLife support: keep your crew aliveManage oxygen, air and water to keep astronauts safe.
  • 02MossEnergy systems: power the missionSolar panels, batteries and smart choices keep it running.
  • 03NoriFood and agriculture: grow beyond earthGrow crops in a greenhouse and keep the crew fed.
  • 04LumaSpace safety: shield your crewSpot space weather and shield astronauts from radiation.
  • 05PipEngineering: build the best baseDesign, test and repair a reliable outpost.
  • 06SkimSustainability: make every source countRecycle and stretch limited resources.
  • 07PebbleMission planning: think aheadSet priorities and prepare for the unexpected.
  • 08TankMultitasking: the final commandsPut everything you learned to the test.

How every mission works

  1. Learn. A crew member teaches one short idea.
  2. Do. Find the tools, reach the equipment and fix it.
  3. Follow the trail. Captions and a glowing path show the next step.
  4. Check. A quiz locks in what you learned.
