THE CHALLENGE

Building a junior astronaut mission trainer

The problem

Space exploration is often taught in one of two ways: oversimplified to the point that students miss important concepts, or explained in language too technical for younger learners to understand.

As STEM students ourselves, we believe learning should strike a balance between accessibility and depth, making complex concepts both understandable and engaging.

The solution

To bridge this gap, we developed Aquanauts, an interactive astronaut simulation game that brings real-world space exploration challenges to life.

Through hands-on missions inspired by NASA data, students navigate realistic scenarios that astronauts have faced or could encounter in space, developing decision-making skills along the way.

THE LESSONS

8 characters, 8 lessons, 8 levels, 8 certifications

  • 01BloopLife support: keep your crew alive
  • 02MossEnergy systems: power the mission
  • 03NoriFood and agriculture: grow beyond earth
  • 04LumaSpace safety: shield your crew
  • 05PipEngineering and infrastructure: build the best base
  • 06SkimSustainability: make every source count
  • 07PebbleMission planning: think ahead of schedule
  • 08TankMultitasking: the final commands

Players walk a 3D lunar base with a keyboard or touchscreen. Each lesson starts with a short idea from a crew member, then players apply it: find the tools, reach the equipment and repair it, like a broken air filter.

Captions and a glowing trail guide the way, and every mission ends with a knowledge check. By the final mission, students use everything they have learned to keep their crew safe.
