About the game

How the game works

Kids play as an astronaut living in the Aquanauts' world. The sea creature crew runs a base on the Moon, and each lesson gives you a problem to solve together.

1

Meet the crew

The crew lives and works on the Moon base. Every character has a different job.

2

Play a lesson

Read the problem at the Moon base, think it through and decide what to do.

3

Move on

Finish a lesson to earn a certificate and unlock the next one.
